Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version: Abyss only keeps what it needs to sync your library between your devices. It has no ads, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Who we are

Abyss is an unofficial, fan-made music player. It is not affiliated with Juice WRLD, his estate or his record labels. Questions about this policy go to ejeheeeeuueue@gmail.com.

Using Abyss without an account

You can use Abyss without signing in. Your playlists, likes, history, covers and settings then stay on your own device and are not sent to us.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Your email address, to identify your account and send sign-in and password-reset emails.
  • Your password, which is handled by our account provider (Supabase) and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Abyss itself never sees or stores it.
  • If you use Continue with Google: your Google account email and basic profile (name and picture) that Google shares when you agree. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your library, so it can sync: playlists, liked songs, listening history and play counts, chosen covers and song names, lyric timing tweaks, and some app settings.

Songs you add from your own computer or phone ("local files") are never uploaded. They stay on that device.

Shared fan covers

When you pick a fan-made cover for a song, the song's ID and the image link are saved to a shared list so other users can see popular covers. This list is not linked to your account or email.

Services Abyss uses

  • Supabase stores accounts, synced libraries and shared covers.
  • Google handles Continue with Google if you choose it.
  • juicewrldapi.com provides the song list and audio. Your device connects to it directly to play music.
  • LRCLIB (lyrics), Apple iTunes Search (official covers) and Serper (fan-made cover search, only with your own key) are searched with song names.
  • Discord: if you turn on the Discord status, the song you're playing is shown on your Discord profile through the Discord app on your device.
  • GitHub hosts app updates. Abyss checks it for new versions.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and synced library until you delete your account. One-time sign-in codes are deleted within 10 minutes.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from Settings. Your music stays on your device.
  • To delete your account and everything synced to it, email ejeheeeeuueue@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Turn the Discord status off in Settings, under Discord.

Children

Abyss is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

Connections are encrypted, and each account can only read its own synced data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention it in the app's patch notes.
